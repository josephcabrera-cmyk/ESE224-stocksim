Algorithm Description :
The strategy compares the current monthly close on the first trading day against the prior 12 monthly closes. 
If the price is down at least 14.2% from the "rolling high", it invests 3 times the normal monthly capital. 
If the price increases at least 5.5% from the "rolling low", it reduces the monthly spend by half. Leftover money 
is carried over, and the final month uses the remaining budget.
